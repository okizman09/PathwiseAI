# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 2026**

Welcome to Pathwise AI. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Information**: When you register, we collect your email address and authentication identifiers provided through Supabase Auth (or Google OAuth).
- **Workspace Data**: Goals, product descriptions, ideas, and workflow generation history you create within the platform.
- **Usage Metrics**: Daily quota consumption, interaction events (such as step completions and tool clicks), and server request logs for rate limiting and platform reliability.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To deliver personalized AI-powered creation paths, prompt templates, and vetted tool recommendations.
- To enforce fair usage limits, prevent abuse, and safeguard server availability.
- To improve our tool matching algorithms and service quality.
- We do not sell your personal data or provide it to third parties for targeted advertising.

## 3. Tool Recommendations & Links
Pathwise AI indexes independent software tools and platforms. Some outbound links may contain signed referral identifiers. We do not transmit your private prompts or project briefs to third-party tools.

## 4. Data Retention & Account Deletion
You retain complete ownership over your data. You may permanently delete your account, saved workflows, generation history, and associated profiles at any time via the "Delete account" feature in the application header or settings menu.

## 5. Security & Service Providers
We implement strict token authentication, rate limiting, and encrypted transport (TLS). Third-party infrastructure providers include Supabase (database & authentication) and Google Gemini (language model processing).

## 6. Contact & Inquiries
If you have questions about this policy or wish to exercise data rights, reach out to us at privacy@pathwise.ai or through our community feedback channels.
